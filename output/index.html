Design and trial implementation of a continental-scale, kilometre-resolution hourly precipitation analysis for Australia using satellite, radar and gauges
Yuhang Zhang, Quan J. Wang, Andrew J. Frost, Jayaram Pudashine, Blair Trewin, Carlos Velasco-Forero, Chun-Hsu Su, and Vincent Villani
Hydrol. Earth Syst. Sci., 30, 6701–6730, https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-30-6701-2026, 2026
This study develops and tests a new system that combines satellite, radar, and rain gauge measurements to produce hourly rainfall maps at 2 km resolution across Australia. The results show improved rainfall estimates and better representation of local rainfall patterns compared with existing products. The system can support real-time rainfall monitoring and reconstruction of historical rainfall, and provides a practical approach that could also be applied in other regions.
